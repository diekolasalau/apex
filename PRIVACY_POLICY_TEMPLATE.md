### A. Legal Reasons
We must collect and keep student records under:
- **Education Act 1996** - Schools are required to maintain student records
- **Data Protection Act 2018** - Schools have statutory authority to process data
- **Children Act 1989 & 2004** - Duty to safeguard children's welfare

- To provide your child with education

- To communicate with you about your child's progress

- To identify and respond to any welfare concerns

## 5. OUR LEGAL BASIS FOR USING YOUR DATA

### A. Why We Can Process Your Data

Under UK GDPR Article 6, we process data based on:

| Basis | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| **Legal Obligation** | Required by law to keep education records (Education Act 1996) |
| **Public Task** | Necessary to exercise official education functions and safeguard students |
| **Legitimate Interest** | Reasonable expectation you'd process routine education data |
| **Consent** | Your agreement for specific uses (e.g., sending daily updates) |

**Note: Your consent is NOT required for core education and safeguarding.** We can process essential data even if you don't consent. However, we ask for your consent for non-essential uses like photography.

### B. Health Data (Special Category Data)

Under UK GDPR Article 10, health/medical data receives extra protection. We process it because:
- **Safeguarding Purposes:** Necessary to protect your child's welfare
- **Educational Purposes:** Necessary to make arrangements for your child's learning

Your medical data is:
- ✅ Encrypted at rest
- ✅ Accessed only by staff who need to know
- ✅ Never shared unnecessarily
- ✅ Treated as highly confidential

## 9. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA

### Data Retention Schedule

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Core enrollment & contact details | Until end of enrollment + **6 years** | Education Act requirement |
| Medical/safeguarding records | Until age 25 + **6 years** | Best practice guidance |
| Communications/consent records | Duration + **2 years** | Compliance & audit trail |
| Access logs/audit records | **2 years** | Security & GDPR accountability |
| Backed-up data | Until primary record deleted + backup schedule | Technical requirement |

### What Happens at End of Retention?

1. **Flagged for Deletion** - On scheduled date
2. **Review** - Check if any ongoing legal/safeguarding reason to retain
3. **Anonymization** (if possible) - May keep anonymized data for statistical purposes
4. **Secure Deletion** - Permanently removed from systems
5. **Confirmation** - Via audit log

### Exceptions (Data Kept Longer)

- Active investigation or court proceeding
- Documented safeguarding concern
- Your request to retain
- Legal hold notice

## 10. YOUR DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS

### Under UK GDPR, you have these rights:

#### A. **Right of Access** (Article 15)
- Request a copy of your personal data
- We must respond within **30 days**
- Free of charge (normally)

#### B. **Right to Rectification** (Article 16)
- Ask us to correct inaccurate data
- We respond within a reasonable time

#### C. **Right to Erasure** (Article 17)
- Request deletion of your data
- **Limited applicability:** We must keep school records by law, so this may be declined
- Safeguarding data may not be deletable

#### D. **Right to Restrict Processing** (Article 18)
- Ask us to limit what we do with your data
- Useful if data disputed or legal case pending

#### E. **Right to Data Portability** (Article 20)
- Request your data in machine-readable format
- **Limited for school records:** Education records kept for child's benefit may be restricted

#### F. **Right to Object** (Article 21)
- Object to processing for direct marketing
- **Limited in school context:** We must process to provide education

#### G. **Right to Withdraw Consent**
- Withdraw any consent given at any time
- Doesn't affect lawfulness of past processing

### How to Exercise Your Rights

**To make a request:**
1. Email: [data.protection@schoolname.edu.uk](mailto:data.protection@schoolname.edu.uk)
2. Or write to: [School Data Protection Officer, Address]
3. Include: Your name, child's name, what you're requesting, and any details

**Response timeframe:**
- Standard: 30 calendar days
- Extension available: +60 days for complex requests (we'll explain why)
- No fee charged (unless request excessive)

### Right to Lodge a Complaint

**If you're unhappy with how we handle data, you can:**
1. Contact us first: [data.protection@schoolname.edu.uk](mailto:data.protection@schoolname.edu.uk)
2. Or complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**:
   - Website: https://ico.org.uk/
   - Phone: +44 (0)303 123 1113
   - Email: casework@ico.org.uk
